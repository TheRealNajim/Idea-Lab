# Product Brief: AI Concept Mashup Generator

## Product Summary
AI Concept Mashup Generator (Idea Lab // Mashup Generator) is an open-source cross-pollination platform that collides two or three orthogonal domains (e.g. Cybersecurity x Cooking, Web3 x Anime, Fashion x Developer Tools) to generate buildable, high-signal, defensible software and hardware concepts.

## Key Authenticated Features
1. Domain Collision Engine: Dual roulette slots + wildcard third domain with randomized dice spin and custom text input.
2. Advanced Experiment Parameters: Fine-tuning Output Format, Build Timeline, Difficulty, Target Audience, System Constraints, and a dynamic Practicality slider (0-100%).
3. Curated Pattern Archive: 40+ production-grade mashup concepts including CipherKitchen, Liquidity Saga, Focus Familiar, and Runway Runtime.
4. Vibecode Prompt Generator: Formatted AI coding prompt with 1-click copy for immediate use in Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Cursor, and ChatGPT.
5. Idea Dossier Deep-Dive: Comprehensive breakdown covering Problem/Solution, Monetization Model, 1-week MVP checklist, Roadmap, and Feasibility/Originality scores.
6. Side-by-Side Comparison: Direct feature and metric comparison between two competing ideas.
7. Saved Ideas Vault: Folder-based categorization (Unsorted, Build Next, Research) and instant Markdown export.
8. Presentation Pitch Mode: Fullscreen minimalist slide deck designed for team pitches and hackathon demos.
9. Zero-Tracking Privacy & Local Engine: Client-side preference ranking and BYOK model selector (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI).

## Visual Direction
Strictly black and white monochrome. Deep obsidian dark mode (#050505, #111111), crisp white typography (#FFFFFF), graphite borders (#262626), high-contrast score meters, subtle grid overlay, and realistic animated macOS/desktop chrome. No color saturation.
