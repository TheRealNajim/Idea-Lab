# Storyboard: AI Concept Mashup Generator

## Video Direction
- Resolution: 1920x1080 @ 30fps (2250 frames).
- Visual Style: Strictly Black & White. Deep charcoal obsidian surfaces (#050505, #111111), fine 1px borders (#262626), stark white typography (#FFFFFF, #E5E5E5), and subtle dot-matrix/grid backgrounds.
- Staging: High-fidelity desktop operating system / workstation perspective. Includes realistic browser window chrome with macOS control pills, navigation URL, subtle glassmorphic blur, fluid cursor motion, and realistic tactile click interactions.
